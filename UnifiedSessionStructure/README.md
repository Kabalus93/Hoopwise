# 酷讯 Unified Session Structure

**Coach handbook for the 90-minute session**
中文版: [README.zh-CN.md](README.zh-CN.md) · Drill import file: [UnifiedSessionDrills.json](UnifiedSessionDrills.json)

Every 酷讯 coach runs every group through the same three 30-minute blocks. The blocks never change. What a coach puts inside them changes with the age group, the roadmap and the group in front of them.

| Block | Minutes | Name | Purpose | Hoopwise section |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–30 | **Warm-up** | Routine dribbling, layups and shooting until they are second nature. Time left over goes to a small competitive game. | Warmup |
| 2 | 30–60 | **Skills** | Review last session's skill, then introduce the next skill on the age-group roadmap if the group is ready. | Skills |
| 3 | 60–90 | **Game** | Regular basketball game. The coach adds one or two constraints that make the kids work the session's skill. | Game |

Hoopwise already defaults every new session to 30 / 30 / 30. Every drill in this program is in the library under the tag `unified-structure`, with a block tag `block-warmup`, `block-skills` or `block-game`.

---

## 1. Why one structure

- **Kids learn the structure once.** From week three a U6 group runs the warm-up routine on its own while the coach corrects. No time is lost explaining what comes next.
- **Repetition builds automaticity.** The routine drills are the same every session. A skill the kids never have to think about in the warm-up is a skill they can use in the game.
- **Every block competes.** Warm-up ends in a game, Skills ends in a 1v1 or 2v2, Game is a game. Competition keeps attention and shows the coach what transferred.
- **Coaches stay free where it matters.** Choice of game, choice of constraint, pace of the roadmap and how to teach a skill belong to the coach. The skeleton does not.
- **Parents and coaches can compare.** Any coach can take over any group and the kids notice nothing. Hoopwise session cards read the same across the club.

## 2. Block 1 — Warm-up (30 min)

### 2.1 The routine (same every session)

Run in this order, one ball per player, full court. Target: 20–24 minutes for the routine, the rest for the game.

| Order | Drill (Hoopwise name) | Minutes | Core content | Added by age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | **Full Court Dribble Progression** | 8 | Regular dribble R/L → skip dribble → in-and-out → alternate (crossover each bounce) | U10+: between the legs, behind the back. U12+: change of pace, retreat dribble |
| b | **Layup Progression** | 8 | No dribble, extended-hand run and layup → no dribble, ball around the waist and layup → dribble basket to basket and layup → with obstacles (cones, chairs) → with a defender | U8/U10: reverse, opposite hand. U12+: euro step, floater, power layup |
| c | **Shooting Routine Ladder** | 6 | One-hand form shooting → two-hand form shooting → three spots with partner rebounding | U10+: catch and shoot off a pass. U12+: one-dribble pull-up |

Rules for the routine:

1. **Never teach in the routine.** The routine is for repetition and individual correction. New content belongs in Block 2.
2. **One correction per player per trip.** The coach walks the lines. Group stops are for safety only.
3. **Both hands, every session.** Weak side gets equal trips and equal layups.
4. **Kids run it themselves** by the third session. The first kid in each line calls the next trip.
5. **Keep the order.** Changing the order resets the automaticity you are building.

### 2.2 Small game when time remains (6–10 min)

Pick one. Criteria: competitive, everyone involved every second, develops passing, court vision or dribbling under pressure.

| Game (Hoopwise name) | Develops | Players | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pass and Tag** | Passing speed, vision, communication | 6–16 | U6–U12 |
| **Police and Thieves** | Dribbling under pressure, eyes up | 6–16 | U6–U12 |
| **Ten Passes** | Passing, spacing, defensive pressure | 6–16 | U8+ (5 passes for U6) |
| **Dribble Knockout** | Ball protection, awareness | 4–16 | U6+ |
| **Sharks and Minnows Dribble** | Change of speed and direction | 6–16 | U6–U10 |
| **Relay Dribble Races** | Routine moves at speed, team competition | 4–16 | U6+ |
| **Red Light Green Light Dribble** | Control, jump stop, reaction | 4–16 | U6–U8 |
| Monkey In The Middle / Bull In The Ring (already in library) | Passing against a defender | 3–6 per group | U8+ |

Rotate games across the month. A game the kids ask for is a game that works; keep it, but not every session.

## 3. Block 2 — Skills (30 min)

### 3.1 Shape of the block

| Part | Minutes | What happens | Hoopwise drill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review | 10 | Last session's skill, run as stations or a point challenge. One player demonstrates first. | **Skill Review Challenge** |
| Introduce | 15 | The next skill on the roadmap, **only if the group is ready**. Otherwise re-teach the reviewed skill with a different drill. | Roadmap drill (tables below) |
| Compete | 5 | The skill under pressure: 1v1, 2v2 or a timed challenge. | **1v1 from Triple Threat**, Give and Go live, etc. |

**Ready rule.** Introduce the new skill when at least half the group executes last session's skill correctly at speed in the review. Below that, the new skill waits. Write the outcome in the Hoopwise coach notes so the next coach knows where the group is.

**Teaching ladder for any new skill.** Demonstrate (20 seconds, one key point) → shadow without a ball → with a ball, no defender → passive defender → compete. Do not skip rungs for young groups. Older groups can start at rung three.

### 3.2 Age-group roadmap

Skills are listed in teaching order. One or two per session, following the Ready rule. The last two columns are the default Compete format for Block 2 and the default constraint for Block 3.

#### U6 · Discovery (4–5 years)

| # | Skill | Hoopwise drill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two-hand catch, ball familiarity, stop on signal | Red Light Green Light Dribble, Partner Passing Series |
| 2 | Jump stop with the ball | Jump Stop and Pivot (stop only) |
| 3 | Triple threat on the catch | Triple Threat Introduction |
| 4 | Finishes from triple threat: regular layup, opposite hand, scoop | Triple Threat Finishes |
| 5 | Chest pass and bounce pass, then on the move | Partner Passing Series |
| 6 | Give and go with the coach as passer | Give and Go |
| 7 | Defensive stance and mirror, no ball | Defensive Stance and Mirror |
| 8 | Front pivot to protect the ball | Jump Stop and Pivot |
| 9 | Form shooting at a low rim | Shooting Routine Ladder (rungs 1–2) |

Compete: **1v1 from Triple Threat**, 2 dribbles, defender's hands behind the back. Game: **3v3 Game: 3-Second Possession** (4 seconds the first weeks), or **Game: Layups Only**.

#### U8 · Introductory (6–7 years)

| # | Skill | Hoopwise drill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Triple threat with jab step | Triple Threat Introduction |
| 2 | Jump stop, front and reverse pivot | Jump Stop and Pivot |
| 3 | Layups with both hands, reverse layup | Layup Progression, Triple Threat Finishes |
| 4 | Crossover under control | Change of Direction Series (round 1) |
| 5 | Give and go 2v1 then 2v2 | Give and Go |
| 6 | Overhead pass, passing on the move | Partner Passing Series |
| 7 | Defensive slides, mirror with a ball | Defensive Stance and Mirror |
| 8 | Shooting form, three spots | Shooting Routine Ladder (rungs 1–3) |
| 9 | Spacing: 2v0 pass and cut with the coach | 3v0 Pass and Cut (U8 variation) |
| 10 | 1v1 reads: attack the front foot | 1v1 from Triple Threat |

Compete: **1v1 from Triple Threat**, 3 dribbles; **Give and Go** live 2v2. Game: **3v3 Game: 3-Second Possession**, **Game: Pass Before You Shoot** (3 passes), **Game: Layups Only**.

#### U10 · Mini (8–9 years)

| # | Skill | Hoopwise drill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Triple threat reads: jab and go, jab and shoot | Triple Threat Finishes, 1v1 from Triple Threat |
| 2 | Change of direction against a passive defender | Change of Direction Series |
| 3 | Catch and shoot footwork: 1-2 step and hop | Catch and Shoot Footwork |
| 4 | Finishing at speed: reverse, opposite hand, first floater | Triple Threat Finishes, Finishing Package (rounds 2 and 4) |
| 5 | Pass off the dribble, push pass | Partner Passing Series (U10 variation) |
| 6 | 3v0 pass, cut and fill → 3v3 | 3v0 Pass and Cut |
| 7 | Give and go with backdoor read | Give and Go |
| 8 | On-ball defense: contain in 1v1, zig-zag slides | Defensive Stance and Mirror |
| 9 | Help position: 2v1 and 3v2 advantage | 2v1 Shooting (library), Around The Cones Transition (library) |
| 10 | Free throw routine | Shooting Routine Ladder (own rung) |

Compete: 1v1 full reads, 2v2, 3v3 half court. Game: **3v3 Game: 3-Second Possession** or 4v4 with **Game: Pass Before You Shoot** (3), periods of **Game: No Dribble**, **Game: Skill Bonus Point**.

#### U12 · Mini (10–11 years)

| # | Skill | Hoopwise drill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Advanced handle: behind the back, hesitation, retreat dribble | Change of Direction Series (rounds 3–5) |
| 2 | Finishing package: euro step, floater, power layup, reverse | Finishing Package |
| 3 | Shooting off movement, one-dribble pull-up | Catch and Shoot Footwork, Shooting Routine Ladder (rung 5) |
| 4 | Free throw routine under a score | Shooting Routine Ladder |
| 5 | Passing: skip pass, hit the cutter, post entry | Partner Passing Series, 3v0 Pass and Cut |
| 6 | Off-ball cuts: V-cut, L-cut, backdoor | Curl Cut Drill, Flare Cut Drill (library) |
| 7 | Defense: closeout, help and recover, deny one pass away | 1 on 1 Closeout, 2v2 Shell (library) |
| 8 | Two-man game: give and go, handoff, pass and screen away | Give and Go, 5v0 Pass & Pick Away (library) |
| 9 | Transition: outlet, fill lanes, get back | Navy Transition, Get Back Drill (library) |
| 10 | Box out basics | Creighton Rebounding (library) |

Compete: 1v1 with 4 dribbles, 2v2 and 3v3 live with constraints. Game: 4v4 or 5v5 with **Game: Pass Before You Shoot** (4), **Game: Weak Hand Only**, **Game: Skill Bonus Point**.

#### U14 · Junior (12–13 years)

| # | Skill | Hoopwise drill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every handle and finish at game speed against live defense | Change of Direction Series, Finishing Package (round 5) |
| 2 | Ball screen use: reject, turn the corner, pocket pass | Ball Screen Reads, 2 on 2 Ball Screens (library) |
| 3 | Shooting off movement with a contest | Catch and Shoot Footwork (U12+ variation) |
| 4 | Finishing through contact | Finishing Package (pad variation) |
| 5 | Reading help: drive and kick, drive and dish | 2v1 Shooting, 4 on 3 Overload (library) |
| 6 | Ball screen defense | Ball Screen Defense Drill (library) |
| 7 | Help rotations and box out | 4v4 Shell Drill, Rebound By Numbers (library) |
| 8 | Transition offense and defense | Disadvantage Drill, Get Back Drill (library) |
| 9 | Free throws under fatigue | Shooting Routine Ladder after conditioning |

Compete: 1v1 live, 2v2 ball screens, 3v3 full court. Game: 5v5 with an 8-second shot clock, **Game: Pass Before You Shoot** (4), **Game: Skill Bonus Point** on the tactical skill of the session.

#### U16 and older · Elite Youth

Same skeleton. The warm-up routine runs at game speed and the shooting ladder becomes a competitive shooting drill with a score. Block 2 follows the team's tactical plan rather than a fixed list. Block 3 uses game-plan constraints: shot clock, scouting rules, situational starts.

## 4. Block 3 — Game (30 min)

### 4.1 Format

| Age | Format | Court | Periods | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U6 | 3v3 | Half court, low rim | 4–5 min | 8 kids: 3v3 plus 2 subs who count the 3 seconds out loud. Rotate every period. |
| U8 | 3v3 | Half court | 4–5 min | Two games at once if two baskets. |
| U10 | 3v3 or 4v4 | Half or full court | 5 min | Full court once both teams get back on defense. |
| U12 | 4v4 or 5v5 | Full court | 5–6 min | Normal rules, coach refereeing. |
| U14+ | 5v5 | Full court | 6–8 min | Shot clock, normal rules. |

Everyone plays the same minutes. Subs always have a job: count passes or seconds aloud, keep score, run a layup line at the free basket.

### 4.2 Constraint menu

Pick one, at most two. Tie at least one to the skill taught in Block 2.

| Constraint (Hoopwise drill where it exists) | Forces | Age |
|---|---|---|
| 3-second individual possession (**3v3 Game: 3-Second Possession**) | Quick decisions, movement, passing | U6–U10 |
| Minimum passes before a shot, 3 or 4 (**Game: Pass Before You Shoot**) | Ball movement, spacing, patience | U8+ |
| No dribble (**Game: No Dribble**) | Pivot, cut, pass, protect the ball | U8+ |
| Layups only (**Game: Layups Only**) | Attacking the rim, cutting, finishing both sides | U6–U10 |
| Weak hand only (**Game: Weak Hand Only**) | Off hand under pressure | U10+ |
| Bonus point for the session's skill (**Game: Skill Bonus Point**) | Transfer from Block 2 | All |
| Every player touches the ball before a shot | Inclusion, spacing | U6+ |
| Jump stop on every catch | Footwork, no travelling | U6–U10 |
| Defense slides only, a reach is a point for the offense | Defensive footwork | U8+ |
| Score only off a cut or a give and go | Cutting, timing | U10+ |
| Make it, take it | Urgency on defense | U10+ |
| 8-second shot clock | Tempo, early decisions | U12+ |

### 4.3 Coaching the game

1. **Count out loud.** Seconds, passes, bonus points. Silent rules do not change behaviour.
2. **Stop play only for a teaching moment under 20 seconds.** Everything else waits for the period break.
3. **Praise the behaviour the constraint targets**, not only the score. A good pass under the 3-second rule gets the same reaction as a basket.
4. **Change the constraint if it kills the game.** If no one can score for a whole period, loosen it or switch.
5. **End on time.** The last two minutes are the Hoopwise log, man of the match and one sentence from the coach on what was good today.

## 5. Worked sessions

### U6 · 8 players · good level (reference session)

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Warm-up 30** | a. Full Court Dribble Progression: regular, skip, in-and-out, alternate. b. Layup Progression: extended-hand run, ball around the waist, dribble basket to basket, then with obstacles. c. Game: Pass and Tag, Police and Thieves, or Ten Passes (5 passes). |
| **Skills 30** | a. Triple Threat Introduction, then Triple Threat Finishes: regular, opposite hand, scoop. b. 1v1 from Triple Threat, 2 dribbles, winner stays. |
| **Game 30** | 3v3 Game: 3-Second Possession. 2 subs count the seconds aloud, rotate every period. |

### U8 · 10 players · session 6 of the term

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Warm-up 30** | Routine a, b, c (layups add the reverse). Game: Relay Dribble Races, last round weak hand. |
| **Skills 30** | Review: Skill Review Challenge on jump stop and pivot. Introduce: Give and Go 2v0 → 2v1. Compete: Give and Go live 2v2, first pair to 3 scores. |
| **Game 30** | 3v3, Game: Pass Before You Shoot (3 passes) with a bonus point for a layup off a give and go. |

### U10 · 12 players · two coaches

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Warm-up 30** | Routine a, b, c in two groups of six, one basket each. Game: Ten Passes, 2 teams of 6. |
| **Skills 30** | Review: Skill Review Challenge on crossover and between the legs at cones. Introduce: Catch and Shoot Footwork, 1-2 step then hop. Compete: pairs, first to 10 makes from the elbow. |
| **Game 30** | 4v4 full court, Game: Skill Bonus Point for a catch and shoot with set feet. Second period: Game: No Dribble. |

### U12 · 10 players · session 2 of a finishing phase

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Warm-up 30** | Routine a, b, c with change of pace and euro step added. Game: Dribble Knockout, two-ball final. |
| **Skills 30** | Review: Skill Review Challenge on euro step. Introduce: Finishing Package rounds 2–4, floater, power, reverse. Compete: 1v1 from Triple Threat, 4 dribbles, 2 points for a finish with the weak hand. |
| **Game 30** | 5v5, Game: Weak Hand Only for the first period, then Game: Pass Before You Shoot (4 passes). |

## 7. Running it in Hoopwise

1. **Build the session.** Create the session from the program phase. Warmup, Skills and Game default to 30 minutes each.
2. **Pick drills by tag.** In each section's drill picker, search `block-warmup`, `block-skills` or `block-game`. The routine drills come first; add one warm-up game. Skills: Skill Review Challenge, the roadmap drill, the compete drill. Game: one constrained game.
3. **During the session** the live banner shows the current block and the time left in it. Switch blocks on the banner, not on your watch.
4. **After the session**, within ten minutes: tick drills completed, set the rating, pick the man of the match, and write coach notes in this form:

```
Reviewed: <skill> — <n>/<total> correct at speed
Introduced: <skill or "none, re-taught …">
Ready to progress: yes / no
Next session: <skill to review> → <skill to introduce>
Game constraint that worked: <constraint>
```

5. **Phases.** When a program uses phases, set each phase's focus to the roadmap stage it covers (for example U8 phase 1: Ball Handling, Passing). The drill picker ranks matching drills higher.
6. **New coaches** install the app and the 28 program drills appear in their library on first launch. For an organisation that has not updated yet, import [UnifiedSessionDrills.json](UnifiedSessionDrills.json) from Drill Library → Import.

## 8. Coach checklist

**Before** · court marked, one ball per player, rims lowered for U6–U8, cones and pinnies out, session built in Hoopwise, constraint decided.

**During** · routine starts on time without a talk · coach moves, never stands · one correction per player · no line longer than four · no coach talk longer than 30 seconds · water at block changes only · every block ends in competition.

**After** · Hoopwise log done within ten minutes · one sentence to the parents who ask: what we reviewed, what we introduced.

## 9. Adaptations

| Situation | Change |
|---|---|
| 4–5 players | Routine unchanged. Warm-up game: Dribble Knockout or Relay Dribble Races. Compete 1v1. Game 2v2 with the coach as permanent passer, or 2v2 plus 1. |
| 12–16 players | Two coaches or two baskets. Split Block 2 by level, not by age. Game: two 3v3 games at once. |
| One basket | Layup Progression runs half court from the sideline. Game 3v3 with the waiting team doing the Shooting Routine Ladder on the side hoop or a wall. |
| 60-minute session | 20 / 20 / 20. Routine: dribble progression and layup progression only. Skills: review 7, introduce 10, compete 3. |
| Group not ready for new skill | Skills block becomes review 15 with a new drill, compete 15. Note it in Hoopwise. |
| Very mixed level | Routine in two lines by level with different added moves. Skills block the same for all, compete paired by level. |
