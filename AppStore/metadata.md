## Description (4000 characters max)

Hoopwise is the ultimate coaching companion for basketball coaches, academies, and sports organizations. Manage your students, plan training sessions, track attendance, and run your programs with ease.

**SMART SESSION MANAGEMENT**
• Plan and organize training sessions with detailed drill breakdowns
• Track warmup, skills, and game segments with customizable durations
• Real-time session status updates and Live Activities support
• Beautiful session cards with location maps and program details

**STUDENT MANAGEMENT**
• Add students with photos, age estimation by birth date or school grade
• Organize students by categories, coaches, and programs
• Track physical measurements (height, weight, position)
• Store parent/guardian contact information

**PROGRAM & CURRICULUM**
• Create structured training programs with phases and micro-cycles
• Assign students to programs with enrollment tracking
• Monitor progress across multiple training phases

**ATTENDANCE TRACKING**
• Quick attendance marking during sessions
• Visual status indicators for present, late, absent students
• Historical attendance records

**LEAGUE MANAGEMENT**
• Organize games and track team performance
• Manage multiple teams and schedules

**MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT**
• Full Chinese (中文) and English language support
• Seamless language switching

**CLOUD SYNC**
• Secure cloud backup with Supabase
• Access your data across devices
• Organization-based data sharing for coaching teams

**DESIGNED FOR COACHES**
• Clean, modern interface optimized for quick access
• Works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac
• Dark mode support
